Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about capturing X posts with PostSnap.

Do I need an X account or API key to use PostSnap?

No. PostSnap operates completely without requiring user accounts, sign-ins, or API tokens. Simply paste any accessible public post link and get your PNG immediately.

Why is the exported PNG sharper than taking a normal screenshot?

Normal screen captures are bound to your display resolution and often capture browser chrome, scrollbars, and compression artifacts. PostSnap renders the post at 2x Retina scale with subpixel typography and clean background boundaries.

Can PostSnap capture private or protected posts?

No. PostSnap respects user privacy and access permissions. Only publicly accessible posts on Twitter/X can be retrieved and captured.

What themes are supported for export?

PostSnap supports Original Light (clean white card), Dim (navy/slate background), and Lights Out (pitch black background), matching X's official theme options.
